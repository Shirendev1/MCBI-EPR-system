## Project Overview

The **MCBI EPR System** is a comprehensive initiative designed to establish an Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) framework within Mongolia's circular economy model. This project focuses on battery waste management, resource recovery, and creating accountability throughout the product lifecycle.

**Key Focus Areas:**
- Battery and waste collection systems
- Manufacturer responsibility frameworks
- Data governance and reporting
- Financial mechanisms for sustainability
- Infrastructure development
- Consumer behavior transformation

---

## What is EPR?

### Definition

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is a policy approach that assigns responsibility to producers (manufacturers and importers) for managing the entire lifecycle of their products, particularly their disposal and recycling. Rather than placing the burden solely on consumers or municipalities, EPR requires producers to fund, organize, and manage end-of-life treatment of their products.

### Core Principles

1. **Producer Accountability**: Manufacturers bear responsibility for their products' environmental impact
2. **Lifecycle Thinking**: Products are managed from creation through end-of-life
3. **Financial Integration**: Producers invest in proper waste management infrastructure
4. **Data Transparency**: Comprehensive tracking and reporting of product flows
5. **Consumer Education**: Awareness campaigns promote responsible disposal

### Global Context

EPR systems have been successfully implemented in:
- European Union (WEEE Directive for electronics)
- Canada (Ontario battery stewardship)
- Japan (Appliance Recycling Law)
- South Korea (Electronics Waste Management)

## Why Mongolia Needs EPR

### Current Challenges

**Waste Management Crisis:**
- Growing battery waste accumulation in urban areas
- Uncontrolled informal recycling operations
- Environmental contamination from toxic materials
- Limited recycling infrastructure
- Lack of standardized collection methods

**Economic Opportunities:**
- Resource recovery and material reuse
- Job creation in collection and processing sectors
- Export value of recycled materials
- Reduced healthcare costs from pollution prevention

**Regulatory Gaps:**
- No comprehensive producer responsibility framework
- Insufficient enforcement mechanisms
- Lack of data transparency on waste flows
- Minimal stakeholder accountability

### Sustainable Development Alignment

The MCBI EPR System aligns with:
- **UN SDG 12**: Responsible Consumption and Production
- **UN SDG 13**: Climate Action
- **Mongolia's Green Development Policy**
- **Circular Economy Transition Goals**

## Project Objectives

### Primary Goals

1. **Establish Legal Framework**
   - Draft EPR legislation for Mongolia
   - Define producer responsibilities
   - Set compliance standards
   - Create enforcement mechanisms

2. **Build Infrastructure**
   - Develop collection networks
   - Establish sorting and processing facilities
   - Create storage and transportation systems
   - Implement secure disposal methods

3. **Create Data Systems**
   - Design tracking and traceability systems
   - Develop standardized reporting formats
   - Implement monitoring dashboards
   - Enable real-time waste flow visibility

4. **Engage Stakeholders**
   - Collaborate with manufacturers
   - Partner with retailers and distributors
   - Involve waste management companies
   - Educate consumers
   - Coordinate government agencies

### Success Metrics

- 80% collection rate for distributed batteries
- 95% proper tracking accuracy
- 100% manufacturer participation
- 50% recycling/reuse recovery rate
- Compliance rates above 90%

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-6)
**Objectives:**
- Legal framework development
- Stakeholder consultation
- Baseline data collection
- System design

**Deliverables:**
- EPR legislation draft
- Stakeholder engagement plan
- Data collection protocols
- Technical specifications

### Phase 2: Pilot Program (Months 7-18)
**Objectives:**
- Test collection systems in 2-3 cities
- Validate data systems
- Train stakeholders
- Refine processes

**Pilot Cities:** Ulaanbaatar, Darkhan, Khövsgöl
**Key Activities:**
- Establish collection centers
- Register 50-100 producers
- Launch consumer awareness
- Conduct system testing

### Phase 3: Scaling (Months 19-36)
**Objectives:**
- Expand to secondary cities
- Increase producer participation
- Optimize operations
- Achieve compliance targets

**Coverage:** 70% of urban population
**Metrics:** 60% collection rate target

### Phase 4: Full Implementation (Months 37+)
**Objectives:**
- National coverage
- Complete stakeholder participation
- Autonomous operation
- Continuous improvement

**Coverage:** 100% of urban areas + major rural centers
**Metrics:** 80%+ collection rate, 95%+ tracking accuracy

## Data Management Framework

### Collection Data

**What We Track:**
- Product type and battery chemistry
- Collection location and date
- Quantity by weight and unit count
- Condition assessment
- Storage duration

**Format:** Standardized MCBI-DM-001 forms

### Processing Data

**Metrics:**
- Input weight vs. output material composition
- Processing efficiency rates
- Recovery percentages by material
- Cost per unit processed
- Environmental parameters (emissions, water use)

### Reporting Schedule

- **Daily:** Collection summaries
- **Weekly:** Processing reports
- **Monthly:** Stakeholder dashboards
- **Quarterly:** Compliance verification
- **Annual:** Comprehensive EPR reports

### Data Security

- Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Access control by role
- Audit trails for all transactions
- Regular backup systems
- GDPR-compliant data handling

## Getting Started

### For Producers
1. Visit the MCBI registration portal
2. Submit company information and product details
3. Calculate contribution amounts
4. Sign compliance agreement
5. Receive MCBI certification

### For Collection Points
1. Complete facility assessment
2. Obtain training certification
3. Install collection equipment
4. Join tracking system
5. Begin operations

### For Consumers
1. Locate nearest collection point (online map)
2. Prepare batteries (do not dispose in trash)
3. Drop off at designated location
4. Receive incentive credits (if applicable)
5. Track collection impact online
